# Pink Horizon 🎀

### Creative & Educational Activities Center for Girls

Pink Horizon Center is a web-based platform designed to introduce parents and guardians to a safe, creative, and educational environment for girls.

The website presents the center's activities, coaches, schedules, and available programs in a clear and organized way, helping parents explore the different opportunities available for their children.

---

## Project Overview

Pink Horizon Center provides information about a variety of creative and educational activities designed for young girls.

The website aims to make it easier for parents and guardians to discover suitable activities, learn about their objectives and requirements, explore available coaches, and view activity schedules.

The platform also includes dedicated dashboards for parents and administrators to support the management and organization of the center's activities.

---

## Site Goal

The primary goal of Pink Horizon Center is to provide parents and guardians with a clear understanding of the unique, creative, and educational programs offered by the center.

The website promotes the center as a welcoming environment where young girls can explore new interests, develop skills, and participate in engaging activities such as:

- Cooking
- Drawing
- Sewing
- Ballet
- And other creative and educational activities

---

## Target Audience

The website is designed for:

- **Young girls aged 3–12 years**
- **Parents and guardians** looking for a safe, engaging, and creative environment for their children


---

## User Experience

The website was designed to provide clear navigation and organized information for parents and guardians.

The interface separates the main areas of the platform into dedicated pages, allowing users to easily navigate between activities, coaches, schedules, and dashboards.

## Project Requirements

The website was developed to support the following main requirements:

| Page | Main Functionality |
|---|---|
| Home | Center introduction, activities, reviews, navigation, and contact information |
| Activities | Activity details, prerequisites, schedules, coaches, and objectives |
| Coaches | Coach profiles and areas of expertise |
| Parent Dashboard | Parent-oriented access and information |
| Administrator Dashboard | Management of children, activities, and coaches |
| Schedule | Weekly coach schedules and availability |
